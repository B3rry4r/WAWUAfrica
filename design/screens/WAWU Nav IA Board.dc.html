Common to all three

Search and notifications live in the header on every screen. Saved, My purchases, DM settings, subscription and earnings all sit inside Profile. Marketplace opens the external Basket and Beauty apps behind a handoff pill — it is never rebuilt in-app. No wallet, no balance, no withdrawal anywhere; credits appear only as a count.
