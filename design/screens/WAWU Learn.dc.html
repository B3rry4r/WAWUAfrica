Ghana

Selling into Ghana

Updated July · 6 min read
Why Ghana first

Ghana is inside ECOWAS with Nigeria, so most agricultural goods cross without duty. It is the cheapest first export a Nigerian producer can make, and the paperwork is a fair rehearsal for harder markets.

What you need
CAC registration and NEPC certificate ECOWAS certificate of origin Phytosanitary certificate for plants
Need the certificate? NEPC registration runs inside WAWU — 14 to 21 days from ₦40,000. Apply for NEPC
