Answer in time, or the money goes back

The inbox is built around the clock, because the clock is the product: reply inside 24 hours and the message pays, miss it and the fan is refunded. Earnings screens are a record — a statement of what was earned. There is no balance to draw down and no withdraw button anywhere.

6 · KYC pending — visible, counted, held
What is deliberately absent

No withdraw button, no available-to-withdraw figure, no transfer, no balance card. These screens report what was earned and where it went — the money moves to the creator's bank account on its own schedule, which is an operations decision, not a control in the app.
