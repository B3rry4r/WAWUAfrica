6 · Where signup ends
User account
Lands in the content feed

Straight into Home with their interests applied. No creator tools anywhere in the shell — no upload, no earnings, no inbox for incoming paid DMs.

Creator account
Lands in creator onboarding, not the feed

Tier choice and payment first, then the ID details. Both gates are independent: paying unlocks uploading and creator tools; the manual ID review unlocks getting paid. Someone can be selling with a review still pending.
